Open a week to see what each lesson builds on, what you will do yourself, and what evidence shows that it worked. Select a lesson title to open it.

How to use the exercises

Start with the named passage under Read before the exercise. Prediction panels have two entry points. You can open the explanation to follow the reasoning, or enter your own answer and check it. For a hardware experiment, run the named case and compare your forecast with your own measurements. Keep the piece of evidence named in the lesson.

Once the guided case works, try Optional challenge · apply it again with the hint closed. You might design an input that exposes an indexing error, explain why a GPU timing contradicts a work estimate, or build a counterexample to your own capstone claim. The third scheduled review brings the same challenge back so you can try it from memory.
