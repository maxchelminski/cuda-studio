The course uses a derived, 512-by-512, four-byte-per-texel tile. Each map was resized
with a Lanczos filter. The channels contain diffuse grayscale (Pillow L conversion),
roughness, red normal and green normal in that order. Pixels are row-major with
the source image's top row first. The byte stream is compressed with zlib and
uploaded once per CUDA module load. The scene tints diffuse intensity differently
for painted walls and wet floors. Its manual wrapped bilinear sampler uses the
signed tangent-space normal XY and the roughness channel directly.
