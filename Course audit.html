What changed for the learner

  • Week 6 now asks you to predict and measure fracture at two thresholds, then follow the tear into the Selvedge studio. The tile-culling exercise starts with a runnable candidate-count experiment. Capstone instructions give concrete evidence to collect for both numerical and graphics projects.
  • The swarm benchmark now checks which segment wins each pixel before timing. Changing the shot clears the viewer’s previous comparison. These fixes were tested with an intentionally wrong segment ID and a camera or material change after a passing comparison.
  • The numerical lab prints individual timing samples in the order collected. CUDA comments containing characters such as the proportionality sign now compile through the Windows Python runner. Failed studio checks stop before they can replace a successful timing file.
  • Review cards show the lesson’s recall prompt and knowledge-check choices. An unreadable saved notebook can be downloaded for recovery, and work entered while browser storage is unavailable can be exported from the current tab.
